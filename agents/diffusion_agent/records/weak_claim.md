# One diffusion claim the evidence cannot support (memo requirement 6)

**Claim:** "56% of colleges already use AI in admissions, and 82% will by 2024." The figures are widely repeated, and the evidence probe's memo uses them as "82% of colleges use AI in admissions, so this is already mainstream."

**Source:** an Intelligent.com survey reported by eCampus News (D20). It was fielded in September 2023 with 399 "education professionals."

**Why it is too weak to place admissions AI, let alone agent-based applicant support, on the curve:**

| Test | Problem |
|---|---|
| Stale | Fielded in September 2023, more than two years before this assessment, for a technology changing every few months. The "by 2024" share was itself a forecast. |
| Population | 21% of respondents worked at private high schools, not colleges. The respondents were individuals, not institutions, so a school may be counted more than once. |
| Sampling | The recruitment method is not disclosed, and no probability sampling is described. It is a commercial survey. |
| Off-application | The uses were mainly reviewing recommendation letters (73%) and transcripts (71%). "Communicating with applicants" (61%) is not defined and does not mean tool-using agents. |
| Conflicts with better evidence | A 2025 survey of 779 administrators reports chatbot use by 36% of admissions and marketing respondents, and **no** agent share (D11). |

**Status in the agent:** grade C. It is named as the weakest claim in every v1.1 output. In cycle 1, v1.0 used it to call the application "early majority" (preserved failure 2).

**Other claims that are too narrow to transfer:**

- **UAE 64% generative-AI use (D3):** geographically narrow, and about individuals, not organizations.
- **31% of developers use agents (D8):** industry-specific lead users.
- **23% of firms "scaling" agents (D5):** self-reported in a global online executive survey, scaling "somewhere"; at most 10% in any given function.

**Most important evidence gap:** no representative measure of tool-using agent adoption exists, either in admissions or in organizations generally.
