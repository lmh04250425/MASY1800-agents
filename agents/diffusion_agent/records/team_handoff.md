# Team handoff: Emerging Technology Diffusion Agent v1.1

1. **Agent package:** `agents/diffusion_agent/`. It contains the instructions, four cases, eight archived outputs, the graded source register and a verifier with 24 checks.

2. **Strongest diffusion signal:** FAQ chatbots in admissions have already crossed.
   - 36% of admissions and marketing respondents use them (D11).
   - NYU itself runs a generative AI admissions bot (D17), and SPS runs Violet (D18).
   - Most young applicants already use generative AI (58% of under-30s have used ChatGPT, D1).

   So the channel and user familiarity exist. Compatibility for a conversational front end is not the problem.

3. **Most important inhibitor:** low observability. No comparable institution has published measured results for tool-using recruitment agents. What is visible is vendor beta skills, announcements and projections (D14, D15). Diffusion is therefore pushed by vendors, not pulled by peers. Data privacy and security, the top barrier in higher education (D11, D12), comes second.

4. **Context-sensitive timing implication.** The evidence is the same in every case. The relevant crossing and the timing differ:

   | Organization | Posture | Relevant crossing | Timing |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | NYU SPS | fast follower | L3b among professional-education peers | Prepare and trial now (sandbox comparison against Violet); no applicant-facing launch on diffusion grounds this cycle |
   | Online provider (synthetic) | early adopter | None required, if pay-off is high and abandonment is acceptable | A bounded, reversible live trial now |
   | Small private college (synthetic) | conservative | L3b among small-college peers, plus CRM vendor references | Wait and monitor; governance work now |

5. **Caution the Adoption Agent should inherit (denominator rule):** "people use generative AI" (L1) and "vendors sell agents" are not evidence that agent-based applicant support has crossed (L3b). Every adoption figure must carry its population, measure, date and geography. Do not compare across populations against the 16% line. Key the go/no-go on L3b peer evidence and on SPS's own comparison against Violet.

**Monitoring for the team:**

- Three or more comparable peers publish measured agent outcomes.
- A higher-education survey reports agent use (not chatbot use) at 16% or more.
- Vendors move recruitment agents from beta to general availability with named references and outcomes.
