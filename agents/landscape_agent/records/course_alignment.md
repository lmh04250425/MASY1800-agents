- **Module 8 (C1):** the classification of applicant support (bleeding edge) is cited from the Adoption specialist and not re-scored. - **Module 6 / A5 (C3):** the denominator rule decides whether "falling behind" can even be claimed (the hype probe). The take-off zone (about 16%) is a trigger threshold.
- **Module 9 preview (C1 slide 54):** the proof of concept stays the place where vendor claims are tested.
