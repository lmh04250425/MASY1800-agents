# Action challenge log (AI-leverage hint 2)

Each action was tested with one question: **does it close a gap observed in this case, or does it only sound like good management practice?** The test was applied to the six v1.0 checklist actions and to the further candidates considered for v1.1.

## The six v1.0 checklist actions, case by case

| v1.0 action | NYU SPS (primary) | High-readiness university (contrast 1) | Low-readiness college (contrast 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secure executive sponsorship | **Recast** as ACT-4. The sponsor exists; what is missing is funded review time (GAP-R5) | **Dropped**: already exists (VP, funded through year one) | **Dropped**: the sponsor already exists |
| Establish an AI governance committee and policies | **Recast** as ACT-5: a review of this agent plus an incident and pause procedure (PARTIAL-R6) | **Dropped**: already exists (committee and templates) | **Dropped as disproportionate**; recast as ACT-3, a short written data rule (GAP-R6) |
| Invest in staff AI training | **Recast** as ACT-6: error-type training after the PoC (PARTIAL-R2) | **Dropped**: already exists (a year of drafting-tool use and a correction process) | **Dropped as premature**: it cannot happen before staff time exists (ACT-1) |
| Develop a change-management plan | **Dropped**: no culture problem observed; R7 is observed in the PoC | **Dropped**: no gap observed | **Recast** as ACT-2: a decision on how saved time is used, answering the stated fear of cuts (GAP-R7) |
| Ensure data quality and system integration | **Recast** as ACT-1: sandbox and approved content for the PoC (PARTIAL-R4) | **Recast** as ACT-1: time the experiment around the CRM migration (PARTIAL-R4) | **Recast** as ACT-5: upgrade the CRM tier or keep booking manual (GAP-R4) |
| Define KPIs and monitor | **Deferred** to the sustain decision at the end of the experiment | **Dropped**: the C4 gates already define the measures; ACT-2 measures review time | **Dropped**: nothing to monitor yet |

**Totals (18 case-action pairs):** 9 dropped, 8 recast into case-specific traced actions, 1 deferred.

## Lesson

A readiness action is only as good as the gap it closes. The most valuable SPS action (naming an owner) does not appear on the generic list at all. Three of the six generic actions (sponsorship, a governance committee, training) describe things the high-readiness university already has.
