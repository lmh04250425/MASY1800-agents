## Interpretation
The meaningful improvement is a narrower claim, not a more dramatic rating. The boundary case changed from Low to an explicit abstention. SPS and provider findings differ because routine support is a cost/support activity for one and the assumed core billable offer for the other. High business exposure does not make the underlying technology a breakthrough.

## Limits of the evaluation
The same Codex conversation generated and reviewed these results. Runs were not blinded, independently scored or repeated across models/seeds. Exact equality of the general paragraph is an intentional invariance control, not a statistical reliability result. This is a small reproducible prompt-and-response diagnostic. It is not validation of a live recruitment agent, proof of ROI, or proof of real-world safety.
