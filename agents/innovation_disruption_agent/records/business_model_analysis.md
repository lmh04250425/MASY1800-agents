# Business-model analysis

## Main judgment
For the proposed NYU SPS workflow, candidate incremental and sustaining process innovation is more defensible than breakthrough or proven disruptive innovation. The provisional business-model judgment is Moderate for recruitment-support activities if meaningful workflow change occurs. It is not a claim that AI replaces degrees, teaching or admissions authority.

| Mechanism | NYU SPS primary case | Hypothetical service-provider contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Existing offer | Education and applicant relationship; public admissions channels verified by S4 | Routine applicant-support hours sold to university clients; scenario assumption |
| Potential created value | Support capacity and easier navigation; no measured enrollment gain | Managed quality, integration and exception handling as a possible new offer |
| Potential displaced value | Repetitive handling and some outsourced routine work | Billable hours and the provider's intermediary role |
| Cost and revenue | Platform, integration, review and correction costs may offset labor capacity gains | Lower delivery effort may also lower billing; savings do not automatically preserve margin |
| Channel and advantage | Direct self-service may matter more; trusted counseling still matters | Direct software purchasing can bypass the provider; accountability can defend it |
| Incumbent incentive | Preserve familiar workflow and trust, but avoid delaying useful learning | Automate to retain clients, but risk cannibalizing current revenue |
| Judgment | Moderate conditional exposure to support activities | High conditional exposure to the assumed core revenue model |
| Counter-case | A simpler FAQ/retrieval workflow achieves the same result | Clients mainly buy trust, difficult-case judgment and accountability |

## Severity and timing
Use the course matrix in C2, slide 24. Primary: Moderate / near-term is a provisional investigation scenario, supporting an evaluation or carefully bounded pilot question. Contrast: High / medium-term is an adaptation-planning scenario, supporting preparation and capability testing. Timing is unverified. For this analysis only, near-term is 0-12 months and medium-term is 1-3 years; these are planning bins, not the lecturer's numeric definitions or measured forecasts. The actual arrival date is withheld. No Potentially Existential conclusion is justified.

## Discriminating evidence
Before a live pilot, compare staff-only, simple retrieval and the proposed agent on the same approved tasks, scored for correct completion, consequential misinformation, escalations and usable staff time. Compare total cost per correctly completed task, including integration, platform, monitoring, human review and correction. The financial identity is total comparable-period operating cost divided by correctly completed tasks; report failures separately so a low success rate cannot look efficient. No savings or enrollment figures are invented.

The agent's claim weakens if the simpler workflow performs equally well at lower cost. The provider's exposure weakens if clients primarily pay for accountable expert relationships. A documented underserved-customer foothold and movement toward incumbent customers would strengthen the disruptive interpretation; those conditions are not yet established.
