# Evidence for and against a Promethean classification of agentic AI

Evidence date: 2026-10-18. Every source was opened on that date. Full citations are in `../evidence/source_register.json`.

## The strongest case FOR (argued in its best form)

Agentic AI turns language models from systems that *draft* into systems that *act*. Independent evaluators find two things:

- Agents' task horizons have doubled roughly every seven months since 2019 (E4).
- Computer-use accuracy jumped from about 5% to 66.3% in a single year, close to the 72.35% human baseline (E5).

Those are order-of-magnitude changes. The underlying models show general-purpose-technology traits: about 80% of US workers have exposed tasks (E3). A shared tool protocol is spreading across ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and developer tools, and has been handed to an industry foundation, which is the kind of catalytic infrastructure the course's criteria describe (E14). Novices gain most from AI assistance, which is a democratizing pattern (E7). Electrification also showed small early effects before factories were rebuilt around it (E2). The instructor's own timeline already lists generative AI as a Potential Promethean (C1, slide 20).

## The strongest case AGAINST

The course instrument asks the suppression question first: if agentic AI vanished today, would the old state simply return? The evidence says yes, largely:

- Generative AI as a whole assisted only 1-5% of US work hours in late 2024 (E6).
- Only 31% of developers, the leading user group, use agents (E13).
- Danish earnings and hours show precise null effects two years after ChatGPT (E9).
- Gartner forecasts that over 40% of agentic projects will be cancelled, and judges most "agentic" vendors to be relabelled products (E12).

In the field, the best agent completed 30% of realistic workplace tasks (E11). Experienced developers were 19% *slower* with AI tools while believing they were faster (E8). A task-based macro estimate puts the 10-year productivity gain below 0.66% (E10). By C1's own rule ("if the old state would simply return ... at most a breakthrough"), agentic AI cannot yet be classified above Breakthrough.

## Judgment

- **Classification:** Breakthrough, moderate confidence.
- **Potential-Promethean candidacy:** UNRESOLVED, low confidence.

The case FOR is a strong argument about trajectory. The case AGAINST is a strong argument about the present. The course instrument decides on the present and leaves trajectory to the mode declaration, so the honest result is a Breakthrough floor with an open, monitored ceiling.

**What would change this judgment:**

- *Upward:* dependence evidence, meaning a rising share of work hours run through agents, or organizations unable to revert; or a second tier visibly reorganizing.
- *Downward:* capability series that plateau across two annual reports, or field studies that keep finding null or negative effects.
